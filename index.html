⚙️ Settings

📅 Your Calendars

Paste a Google Calendar embed URL, public iCal link, or just the email address. Each calendar must be set to public in Google Calendar settings.

Example:
• https://calendar.google.com/calendar/embed?src=name@gmail.com&ctz=...
• schreinerfamilycalendar@gmail.com
• Sports: search "Interesting calendars" in Google Calendar to subscribe, then add the email here

  1. In Google Calendar, click the ⋮ next to a calendar → "Settings and sharing"
  2. Under "Access permissions" → check "Make available to public"
  3. Scroll to "Integrate calendar" → copy the "Calendar ID" (email) or the "Public URL"
  4. Paste it above and click + Add
  5. Sports calendars and holidays are already public — just add them!

📱 Android Tablet Wall Display Tips

  1. Open this page in Chrome on your Android tablet
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu → "Add to Home screen" or "Install app" — runs full-screen!
  3. Go to Settings → Display → Screen timeout → 30 minutes (or use a stay-awake app)
  4. Optional: Enable Developer Options → Stay awake (screen never sleeps while charging)
  5. Optional: Use Screen Pinning (Settings → Security → Screen pinning) to lock to this app
